# Cubes (1 to 20)

Cubes show up in series questions and in volume and algebra problems.

## The table

| n | cube | n | cube | n | cube | n | cube |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1³ | **1** | 6³ | **216** | 11³ | **1331** | 16³ | **4096** |
| 2³ | **8** | 7³ | **343** | 12³ | **1728** | 17³ | **4913** |

## Shortcuts

- **Next cube:** (a + 1)³ = a³ + 3a² + 3a + 1. Example: 12³ = 11³ + 3×121 + 3×11 + 1 = 1331 + 363 + 33 + 1 = **1728**.
- **Last digit of a cube** (same digit comes back for 1, 4, 5, 6, 9 and 0):

| Number ends in | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cube ends in | 1 | **8** | **7** | 4 | 5 | 6 | **3** | **2** | 9 | 0 |

  (2↔8 and 3↔7 swap.)
- **Tens trick:** (10a)³ = a³ followed by 000. So 20³ = 8000, 30³ = 27000.
- **Cube of a square root link:** 4³ = 64 = 8², 9³ = 729 = 27², 16³ = 4096 = 64². Perfect sixth powers are both squares and cubes.
