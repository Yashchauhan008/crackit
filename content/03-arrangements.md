# Arrangements

Seating puzzles are a large part of RRB / IBPS Reasoning. You are given a short paragraph of conditions, then 3–5 questions on the final layout.

## Linear row

- People sit in a straight line. Unless told otherwise, **all face North**.
- **Left / right** are from the person’s point of view (same as left/right on the page when everyone faces North).
- “A sits second to the right of B” means: from B, skip one seat, then A.
- “Three persons sit between A and B” does **not** tell you who is on the left — try both sides until other clues lock it.

## Circular table

- Seats go around a circle. Diagrams here place seat 1 at the **top** and number **clockwise**.
- **Facing the centre (inside):** left/right are from each person’s view looking inward. On the top-view diagram that means **right = anti-clockwise** and **left = clockwise**.
- **Facing outside:** left/right reverse (**right = clockwise**, **left = anti-clockwise**).
- “Opposite” only exists cleanly when the seat count is even (6, 8, …).

## Dual facing (N/S or in/out)

- Some face one way, some the other. Mark facing on every seat as soon as a clue gives it.
- Neighbours “facing opposite to X” is a strong lock — place X first, then flip the neighbours.

## How to use Solve in this app

1. Open a puzzle under **Practice → Arrangements**.
2. Read the passage, attempt the MCQs.
3. Press **Solve** → **Play** (or step with Next / Prev).
4. Watch each **condition** place people on the diagram. Newly placed seats pulse; highlighted seats are the ones the clue talks about.

Start with the short **Original** teaching sets, then try the **RRB PO 22 Nov 2025** memory-based sets.
