## Shortcuts

- **Next square:** (a + 1)² = a² + 2a + 1. Example: 24² = 23² + 2×23 + 1 = 529 + 47 = **576**.
- **Ends in 5:** (10a + 5)² = a × (a + 1) followed by 25. Example: 25² → 2×3 = 6 → **625**; 35² → 3×4 = 12 → **1225**.
- **Near 10, 20, 30:** (30 − b)² = 900 − 60b + b². Example: 28² = 900 − 120 + 4 = **784**.
- **Last digit of a square:** only 0, 1, 4, 5, 6, 9 are possible. A number ending in 2, 3, 7 or 8 is never a perfect square.
- **Pairs that add to 10 share a last digit:** 13² = 169 and 17² = 289 both end in 9; 12² = 144 and 18² = 324 both end in 4.
